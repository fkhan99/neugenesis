What we're building

Simulation

Model interventions together

Treatments rarely act alone. Our tools let researchers study combinations and schedules as a whole, rather than one variable at a time.

Uncertainty

Carry the error bars through

Every result arrives with its spread. Where a conclusion depends on a number nobody has measured, the software says so plainly.

Design

Point to the next experiment

The most useful output is often a question. We surface which measurement would most sharpen a decision, and what it would take to run it.

Our approach

Useful because it's honest about its limits.

Research software earns trust by being candid about what it cannot answer. Ours is built to generate hypotheses and help design experiments — not to predict clinical outcomes, and not to replace judgement.

When the evidence can't separate two options, we'd rather say so than manufacture a winner.
